Scan the QR code, tap Start.
Your phone is on your computer.

HopScreen mirrors the whole screen of your iPhone or Android phone to a window on a Mac or Windows PC on the same Wi-Fi. It is free, with no ads.

How it works

  1. Open the HopScreen receiver on your computer. It shows a QR code for connecting.
  2. In HopScreen on your phone, tap “Scan QR” (QRを読む) and scan the code on your computer.
  3. Tap “Start” (スタート) and allow screen sharing. The first time a phone connects, click “Allow” (許可) on the computer.

To stop, tap “Stop” (停止) on your phone or close the window on your computer.

Features

No cables, no setup

No IP addresses to type. Just scan the QR code.

Phone to computer, directly

Video never goes through a server on the internet. It travels straight to your computer over your Wi-Fi.

Encrypted, and you approve

The connection is encrypted. A new phone can’t show anything until you allow it on the computer.

Several phones at once

Each phone gets its own window on the same computer.

Requirements

  • Phone: iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or Android 10 or later
  • Computer: Mac with macOS 14 or later, or Windows 10/11
  • The phone and the computer must be on the same Wi-Fi (the same network)
  • Language: the apps are currently in Japanese only

Audio is not sent yet. Notifications that arrive while you are sharing also appear on the computer, so you may want to turn on a Focus mode (Do Not Disturb).
