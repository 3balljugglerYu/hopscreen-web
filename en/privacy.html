Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

HopScreen (“the app”) mirrors the screen of a phone (iPhone or Android) to a computer (Mac or Windows) on the same network. The app is provided by Hidechika Doi, an individual developer. This page explains what information the app — the phone app and the computer receiver — handles. If this English version and the Japanese version differ, the Japanese version prevails.

In short

  • The app sends no information to any server run by the developer. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
  • Your phone’s screen is sent, encrypted and directly over your local network, only to the computer whose QR code you scanned.
  • Mirrored screens are not saved on the phone or on the computer.

What the app handles and why

Screen video

When you tap “Start” (スタート) and allow screen sharing, the app captures the whole screen of your phone (including notifications that arrive while sharing), compresses it and sends it to the computer you chose with the QR code. It does not go through any server on the internet. The connection is encrypted with TLS, and the app checks the computer’s certificate fingerprint to make sure it is the computer that showed the QR code. The video is not saved.

Device information sent from the phone to the computer

When connecting, the phone sends the following to the computer. The computer uses it for the approval prompt and for its list of allowed devices.

  • Device name, model and OS version
  • An identifier for the device (on iPhone, the app-specific identifierForVendor; on Android, ANDROID_ID)
  • A device key (a random value created by the app, used to show that the device was allowed on that computer)

Information stored on the computer

  • List of allowed devices: device name, model, device identifier, a hash of the device key (the key itself is not stored), when it was allowed and when it last connected. You can remove devices under “Registered devices” (登録済みの端末) in the receiver.
  • Connection statistics: numbers such as delay and data rate are written, with the device name, to files on the computer (on Mac, ~/Library/Logs/HopScreen). They contain no video.
  • The computer’s certificate and key used for encryption.

Information stored on the phone

The app stores the last computer you scanned (addresses, port, computer name, certificate fingerprint and one-time code) and a device key for each computer. On iPhone these are kept in the Keychain; on Android, in the app’s data.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan a computer’s QR code inside the app. The app neither saves nor sends camera images.

  • iPhone: scanning uses Apple’s VisionKit and happens on the device.
  • Android: scanning uses the Google Play services “code scanner” (Google ML Kit). Scanning happens on the device and the app only receives the result. According to Google, images and scan results are not stored by Google. However, this component sends information such as device model, OS version, app name and version, and processing times to Google over an encrypted connection, for diagnostics and usage analytics (Google’s description).

Local network

Used to connect to the computer on the same network. On iPhone, the app asks for “Local Network” permission.

Notifications (Android)

While sharing, a notification shows that sharing is on and offers a stop button.

Sharing with third parties

Apart from the Google component above (QR scanning on Android), the app sends no information to third parties. Information is never sold.

Deleting information

  • Phone: deleting the app deletes its data. On iPhone, however, the saved computer and device keys in the Keychain may remain after the app is deleted.
  • Computer: remove devices under “Registered devices” (登録済みの端末) in the receiver. Statistics are removed by deleting the files above.

About this website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub handles access logs (GitHub General Privacy Statement).

Changes to this policy

If the information the app handles changes, this page will be updated along with the “Last updated” date.
