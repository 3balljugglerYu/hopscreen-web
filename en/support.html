FAQ

It won’t connect, or nothing shows up
  • Make sure the phone and the computer are on the same Wi-Fi (the same network).
  • Make sure the HopScreen receiver is running on the computer.
  • On iPhone, turn on “Local Network” in Settings → HopScreen.
  • Guest, office or school Wi-Fi often blocks devices from talking to each other. Try your home Wi-Fi or a personal hotspot.
  • Allow HopScreen in the computer’s firewall or security software.
It says a new QR code is needed

A phone connecting for the first time needs the QR code currently shown on the computer. Each code works once, for 5 minutes. Scan the code on the computer again with “Scan QR” (QRを読む), then tap “Start” (スタート).

It says the computer couldn’t be verified

The app couldn’t confirm that it reached the computer that showed the QR code. This happens when the computer’s encryption certificate was recreated, or when the computer’s address changed and another device now uses it. Scan the QR code on the computer again.

The computer asks whether to allow a connection

When a phone connects for the first time, the computer asks you to “Allow” (許可) or “Deny” (拒否). Click “Allow” if you recognize it; that phone will connect without asking from then on. If nobody answers within 60 seconds, the connection is denied.

How do I revoke a phone I allowed?

In the Mac receiver, choose “Registered devices…” (登録済みの端末…, ⌘D) from the menu, select the phone and click “Remove” (削除). If that phone is mirroring, it stops.

The camera doesn’t work for “Scan QR” (iPhone)

Turn on “Camera” in Settings → HopScreen. You can also scan the computer’s QR code with the iPhone’s Camera app; it opens HopScreen.

Notifications or private screens show up on the computer

While sharing, the whole phone screen is mirrored, including notifications. Turning on a Focus mode (Do Not Disturb) helps.

Is audio supported?

Not yet. Only the screen is sent.

How do I stop?

Tap “Stop” (停止) on the phone, or close the window on the computer.
